About AST Education

AST Education is the working name of ALL STUDIES & TECHNICAL EDUCATION, a micro enterprise based in Roorkee, Uttarakhand.

The business was established on 5 December 2023 and registered on Udyam on 27 December 2023. It is linked with SUMAN EDUCATIONAL TRUST as its parent organisation.

Educational services

School coaching, higher education coaching, computer education, technical skill development and personality development.

Coaching, academic support and degrees

AST Education provides coaching and academic support. It does not award university degrees. Degrees are awarded by the universities that conduct the relevant programmes.
